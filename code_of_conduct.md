# Community Guidelines & Code of Conduct

The vCluster community is built around collaboration, learning, openness, and respect.

We want the community to be a place where developers, contributors, maintainers, ambassadors, and community members can participate safely and confidently regardless of background, experience level, identity, or geography.

These guidelines apply across all community spaces, including:

- Online communities and discussion platforms
- Social media interactions
- Events, meetups, workshops, and conferences
- Livestreams and virtual sessions
- One-on-one interactions within the ecosystem
- Ambassador and contributor activities

Everyone participating in the vCluster community is expected to contribute to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment.

## Expected Behavior

We expect community members to:

- Be respectful and professional
- Welcome new contributors and learners
- Communicate constructively and respectfully
- Be open to different viewpoints and experiences
- Help create a safe and inclusive environment
- Support collaboration, learning, and healthy discussion
- Give feedback honestly but respectfully
- Act in good faith toward other community members

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not tolerated within the vCluster community:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination in any form
- Offensive comments related to gender, identity, sexual orientation, disability, race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, age, or protected characteristics
- Personal attacks, bullying, or targeted hostility
- Deliberate disruption of talks, events, discussions, or community spaces
- Unwanted sexual language, imagery, or advances
- Stalking, intimidation, or invasive behavior online or in person
- Trolling, bad-faith engagement, or repeated disrespectful conduct
- Sharing private information without consent
- Any behavior that makes community members feel unsafe, unwelcome, or excluded

## Reporting & Enforcement

If you experience or witness behavior that violates these guidelines, please report it to the vCluster team or community moderators Hrittik Roy (@hrittikhere) or Saiyam Pathak (@saiyam1814).

Reports will be reviewed seriously and handled with discretion.

Depending on the situation, actions may include:

- Private warnings
- Removal from community spaces or events
- Revocation of ambassador status
- Temporary or permanent bans from community participation
- Escalation to event organizers or appropriate authorities if required

## A Community Built Together

The best open source communities are built by people who teach, share, collaborate, test, give feedback, and help others grow.

We want the vCluster ecosystem to remain welcoming, technically strong, community-driven, and accessible to developers everywhere.
